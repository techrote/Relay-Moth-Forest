RELAY MOTH FOREST 3.99 — GRAPHICS / LIGHTING CONTROLS
=====================================================

GRAPHICS MENU
-------------
The Graphics + Lighting panel is a compact left-anchored floating editor. It does not
apply CSS backdrop blur to the game. All scalar values are manually editable number
fields rather than sliders.

AMBIENT LIGHT PROGRESSION
-------------------------
Entering a room resets its ambient target to AMBIENT INITIAL. Once all objectives in
that room are complete, ambient lighting eases toward AMBIENT COMPLETE. Press B during
normal play to toggle the global force-complete-light override.

BUMP / SHADOW ORDER
-------------------
The bump atlas is coordinate-matched to the colour atlas and deliberately low-frequency
to avoid shimmering highlights. Projected shadows and contact AO are rendered before
live character sprites, so character artwork is never darkened by its own shadow layer.
