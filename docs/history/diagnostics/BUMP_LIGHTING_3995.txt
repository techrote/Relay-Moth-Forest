RELAY MOTH FOREST 3.995.1 — BUMP / MATERIAL LIGHTING
==================================================

Shipping defaults remain:
    bumpStrength  1.15
    specular      0.28
    aoStrength    0.18

The colour and bump atlases remain coordinate-identical. Height response is deliberately
prefiltered/low-frequency; the HD sprite shader samples neighbouring height texels with a
2.35-texel footprint and broad low-energy specular response to avoid the granular shimmer
that earlier experiments produced on moving sprites.

SurfaceFX does not change sprite bump authority. WaterField computes its own coherent
analytical normals, while GrassField uses simple instanced geometry and LUT-driven colour.
Projected shadows/contact AO remain below live character sprites.
