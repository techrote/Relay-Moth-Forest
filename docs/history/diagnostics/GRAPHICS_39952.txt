POST SEMANTICS
Brightness is an additive linear-light offset after exposure/light/bloom composition. Contrast
is centered around mid-grey. Gamma is a final transfer adjustment independent of exposure.
Temperature and tint bias final channel balance; shadow lift and highlight gain provide simple
tonality shaping without replacing semantic LUT colour selection.
