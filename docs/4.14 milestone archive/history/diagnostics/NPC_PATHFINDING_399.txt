RELAY MOTH FOREST 3.99 — NPC PATHFINDING / FAULT FIX
=====================================================

FAULT ROOT CAUSE
----------------
The follower and mini-robot update faults reported as:
    undefined is not iterable (cannot read property Symbol(Symbol.iterator))
were caused by no-route pathfinding results. Older code could reduce the active
waypoint index to -1 and then destructure path[-1].

3.99 FOLLOWER POLICY
--------------------
Full-size recruited robots retain collision-aware room navigation. Every path result
is guarded before use. If a route cannot be achieved, recovery is deliberately local:

    attempt 1  short step in a clear cardinal direction
    attempt 2  short randomized clear cardinal/diagonal step
    attempt 3  another randomized clear step

The robot then retries navigation. Long-range recovery teleport remains a last resort.
The fallback does not throw and never destructures a missing waypoint.

3.99 MINI-ROBOT / WILDLIFE POLICY
---------------------------------
Mini robots and woodland creatures are hint/ambience actors, not collision actors.
They use low-tick direct waypoints and intentionally ignore the map collision graph.
This removes unnecessary BFS work and prevents a decorative NPC from faulting the
player simulation.
