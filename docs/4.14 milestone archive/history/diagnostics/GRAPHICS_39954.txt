3.995.4 WATER NOTE
Idle water is a bounded analytical pseudo-spectrum: qualitative long/medium/short directional
wave bands use deep-water dispersion and analytic gradients. There is no per-frame FFT, no
float ping-pong simulation and no SSR/physics-foam chain. Interaction splashes remain bounded
transient ripple sources. Projected shadows are masked out over exposed water.

3.995.4 GRASS NOTE
All nine rooms contain explicit authored `grass_clumps`. Grass remains visual-only and instanced;
it never changes Room collision/path authority.
