RELAY MOTH FOREST 3.995.2 — BUMP / LARGE-SPRITE / SHADOW LIGHTING
=================================================================

Current shipping bump default: 1.15. Specular default: 0.28. Coordinate-matched colour and
bump atlases remain the source of HD pseudo-normal response.

Large trees now use a single logical scale and source UV basis: the complete original tree is
rendered in the live HD material pass, and foreground canopy occlusion is cropped from the same
full region after actors. This removes the previous independent trunk/canopy scaling path and
keeps height/neighbour samples continuous across the whole tree image.

Projected shadows and contact AO originate at explicit ground-contact positions. Shadow width
is classified independently for robots, large decor/trees and small decor/bushes. Shadow/AO
geometry still renders before live actors, so it cannot darken the character sprite placed over
it.
